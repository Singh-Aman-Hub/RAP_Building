# Lumen Cloud Employee Handbook

This handbook applies to every employee of Lumen Cloud GmbH. Policies are reviewed once a year by People Operations. If a rule here conflicts with local law, local law wins.

## 1. Working at Lumen

### 1.1 Working hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your own time zone. Outside core hours you may arrange your day freely. Part-time staff agree their core hours with their manager.

### 1.2 Remote work

#### 1.2.1 Eligibility

Every employee may work remotely up to three days per week after the six-month probation period. Employees in their probation period must be in the office four days per week.

#### 1.2.2 Home office equipment

Lumen pays for a desk setup so that you can work comfortably. The budget is 1,200 euros and it can be spent once every three years. It covers a desk, a chair and a monitor. Receipts must be uploaded to the expense tool within 30 days. Items bought after that date are not reimbursed.

#### 1.2.3 Working from abroad

You may work from another country for up to 20 days per calendar year. Longer stays need written approval from People Operations because of tax and social security rules. It is not allowed to work from a country on the sanctions list.

## 2. Time off

### 2.1 Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 28 days of paid annual leave per year. Unused days carry over until 31 March of the next year. After that date they expire.

### 2.2 Sick leave

Tell your manager before 9:30 on the first day of sickness. A doctor's note is required from the fourth consecutive day. Sick leave is paid in full for up to six weeks.

### 2.3 Parental leave

#### 2.3.1 Primary caregiver

The primary caregiver receives 18 weeks of fully paid leave. It can start up to four weeks before the due date. The leave must be taken within the first 12 months after the birth or adoption.

#### 2.3.2 Secondary caregiver

The secondary caregiver receives 6 weeks of fully paid leave. These weeks may be split into two blocks. Each block must be at least two weeks long.

#### 2.3.3 Returning to work

After the leave, employees can return part-time for up to three months. During this time they keep their full salary for the hours they work and half of the remaining hours. Please tell your manager at least eight weeks before you return.

## 3. Expenses and travel

### 3.1 Travel booking

Book flights and trains through the travel portal. Economy class is the default for flights under six hours. Flights longer than six hours may be booked in premium economy. Business class is never reimbursed, except with a written exception from the CFO.

### 3.2 Meals and per diem

When travelling for work, the daily meal allowance is 45 euros in Europe and 70 euros outside Europe. Alcohol is not reimbursed. Team dinners are limited to 60 euros per person.

### 3.3 Submitting expenses

Submit expenses in the expense tool within 30 days. Managers approve within five working days. Finance pays approved expenses with the next monthly payroll run. Expenses above 500 euros need a second approval from the department head.

## 4. Information security

### 4.1 Passwords and devices

Use the company password manager for every work account. Passwords must be at least 14 characters. Laptops must use full-disk encryption and lock after five minutes of inactivity. Report a lost laptop within one hour to the security team at security@lumen.example.

### 4.2 Incident reporting

If you suspect a data breach, do not try to fix it alone. Report it immediately. The on-call security engineer decides whether the incident is declared. A declared incident gets a severity from SEV1 to SEV4.

```bash
# Report a suspected incident from the terminal
lumen-cli incident report --severity SEV2 --summary "Suspicious login from unknown country"
# The command prints an incident id such as INC-20481
```

The incident id must be quoted in every message about the incident. Severity SEV1 means customer data is exposed or the platform is down for most customers.

### 4.3 Acceptable use

Company devices are for work, but occasional private use is fine. Do not install software that is not on the approved list. It is reviewed every quarter by the security team.

## 5. Learning and development

### 5.1 Learning budget

Every employee has a learning budget of 1,500 euros per year. It covers courses, books and conference tickets. The budget does not carry over to the next year.

### 5.2 Conferences

Conference travel is paid on top of the ticket. You must share what you learned in a short internal talk within one month after you return.
