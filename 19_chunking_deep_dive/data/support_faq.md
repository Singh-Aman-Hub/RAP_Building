# Lumen Cloud Support FAQ

**Q: How do I reset my password?**
A: Open the console login page, choose "Forgot password" and enter your email. The reset link is valid for 30 minutes.

**Q: Can I change my plan in the middle of a billing month?**
A: Yes. Upgrades apply immediately and are billed pro rata. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing month.

**Q: Which payment methods do you accept?**
A: We accept credit cards and SEPA direct debit. Invoice payment is only available on the Enterprise plan.

**Q: Where is my data stored?**
A: Data is stored in the region you choose when you create a project: Frankfurt, Dublin or Virginia. It never leaves that region unless you copy it yourself.

**Q: How long do you keep deleted projects?**
A: Deleted projects stay in a recovery bin for 14 days. After that they are removed permanently and cannot be restored.

**Q: Do you offer a student discount?**
A: Yes, students get 50 percent off the Pro plan for one year. Send a valid university email address to the support team.

**Q: What is the uptime guarantee?**
A: The Pro plan has a 99.9 percent monthly uptime target and the Enterprise plan has 99.95 percent. Credits are issued automatically if we miss the target.

**Q: How do I add a teammate to my organisation?**
A: Go to Settings, then Members, and send an invitation by email. Invitations expire after seven days.

**Q: Can I get a copy of my invoices?**
A: Yes. Every invoice is available as a PDF under Billing, and the last 24 months are kept online.

**Q: Why was my deployment marked as failed?**
A: Most failures come from a build that runs longer than 20 minutes or from a missing environment variable. Open the build log in the console to see the exact line.

**Q: Is there a phone number for support?**
A: Phone support is offered only to Enterprise customers. Everyone else can use chat or email, and we answer within one working day.

**Q: How do I cancel my account?**
A: Choose Settings, then Account, then "Close account". Your data is exported to a download link that stays active for seven days.

**Q: Do you support single sign-on?**
A: Single sign-on with SAML is available on the Enterprise plan. Setup usually takes about two hours with our team.

**Q: Can I use Lumen for a project with personal data?**
A: Yes. We sign a data processing agreement with every customer on request, and the Frankfurt and Dublin regions are covered by EU data protection law.

**Q: What happens when I reach my monthly request quota?**
A: Requests above the quota are rejected with status 429 until the next month starts, unless you upgrade your plan.
