# Shipping and Delivery

## Delivery times
Orders ship from the Pune warehouse and arrive in 4 working days in most Indian cities. Remote pin codes in the North-East and Ladakh can take up to 9 working days.

## Shipping charges
Shipping is free on orders above 799 rupees. Below that, a flat fee of 49 rupees applies. Express delivery costs 149 rupees and arrives in 2 working days in metro cities only.

## Tracking an order
A tracking link is sent by SMS and email as soon as the parcel leaves the warehouse. The link works for 30 days after dispatch.

## International shipping
We do not ship outside India at the moment. Customers abroad can buy through our partner store on a marketplace, where the partner sets its own prices and delivery times.
