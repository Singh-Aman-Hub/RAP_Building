# Product Catalogue

## Classic blends
Masala Chai (SKU CK-MASALA-250) is a strong Assam black tea with cardamom, ginger, cinnamon and clove. Assam Breakfast (SKU CK-ASSAM-250) is a malty black tea without spices. Both cost 349 rupees for a 250 g pouch.

## Green and herbal teas
Tulsi Green (SKU CK-TULSI-100) mixes green tea with holy basil and costs 299 rupees for 100 g. Chamomile Calm (SKU CK-CHAMO-100) is caffeine free and costs 279 rupees for 100 g.

## Seasonal blends
Monsoon Ginger (SKU CK-MONSOON-250) is sold only from June to September. Winter Kahwa (SKU CK-KAHWA-100), with saffron and almond, is sold from November to February.

## Pack sizes
Classic blends come in 250 g and 1 kg pouches. The 1 kg pouch costs 1,199 rupees and is the best price per gram.
