# Wholesale and Business Orders

## Minimum order
Cafes, hotels and offices can buy wholesale. The minimum wholesale order is 10 kg across any blends.

## Wholesale pricing
Wholesale buyers get 25 percent off the retail price. Orders above 50 kg get 35 percent off. Prices include GST, and a tax invoice is issued for every order.

## Payment terms
New business customers pay in advance. After three paid orders, a business can apply for 30-day credit terms.

## Custom blends
We create custom blends for orders of at least 100 kg per year. Development takes about 6 weeks and includes two rounds of tasting samples.
