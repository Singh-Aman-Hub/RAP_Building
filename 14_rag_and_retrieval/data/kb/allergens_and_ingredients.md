# Allergens and Ingredients

## Nuts
Every blend except Winter Kahwa is nut free. Winter Kahwa contains almond slivers. All blends are packed in a facility that also handles almonds, so traces are possible.

## Sesame and gluten
The packing facility also handles sesame. None of our teas contain gluten, but we do not hold a gluten-free certification.

## Sugar and additives
No blend contains added sugar, artificial flavour or preservatives. The spices in Masala Chai are whole spices ground in-house every week.

## Caffeine
Black and green teas contain caffeine. A cup of Masala Chai has roughly 40 mg of caffeine. Chamomile Calm and Winter Kahwa without green tea are caffeine free.
