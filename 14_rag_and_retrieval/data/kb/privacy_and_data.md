# Privacy and Your Data

## What we collect
We store your name, delivery address, phone number, email and order history. We never store full card numbers; payments are handled by our payment gateway.

## Marketing messages
We send marketing emails only if you opt in. You can unsubscribe with one click from any email, and WhatsApp offers stop when you reply STOP.

## Deleting your account
You can ask support to delete your account. We delete personal data within 30 days, but we keep invoices for 8 years because tax law requires it.

## Data sharing
We share your address and phone number only with our delivery partners, and only to deliver your order. We do not sell personal data.
