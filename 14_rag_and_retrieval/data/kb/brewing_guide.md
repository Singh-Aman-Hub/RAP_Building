# Brewing Guide

## Masala chai on the stove
Boil 1 cup of water with 1 cup of milk. Add 2 teaspoons of Masala Chai and simmer for 4 minutes. Strain and add sugar to taste.

## Green tea
Use water at about 80 degrees Celsius, not boiling, and steep for 2 minutes. Boiling water makes green tea bitter.

## Storage
Keep tea in an airtight container away from sunlight and strong smells. Unopened pouches stay fresh for 12 months; opened pouches are best within 3 months.

## Iced tea
Brew the tea twice as strong as usual, let it cool for 10 minutes, then pour it over ice. Herbal teas make good caffeine-free iced tea for children.
