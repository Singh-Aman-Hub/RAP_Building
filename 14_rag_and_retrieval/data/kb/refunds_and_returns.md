# Refunds and Returns

## Refund window
Refunds at Chai Kitchen are issued within 9 days of delivery if the seal on the pouch is unbroken. Opened pouches cannot be refunded unless the tea arrived damaged. The money is returned to the original payment method within 5 working days after we approve the request.

## How to request a refund
Open the order in your account, choose "Request refund", and upload one photo of the sealed pouch. A support agent reviews every request within 48 hours. You do not need to post the tea back for orders under 500 rupees.

## Damaged or wrong items
If a pouch arrives torn, wet or is the wrong blend, we replace it for free even if it is opened. Report the problem within 72 hours of delivery and include a photo of the outer box.

## Gift orders
Gift recipients cannot request refunds themselves. The person who paid for the gift must start the request from their own account.
