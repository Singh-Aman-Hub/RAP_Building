# Loyalty Programme

## Earning points
Customers earn 3 points for every 100 rupees spent. Points are added to the account 10 days after delivery, once the refund window has closed.

## Redeeming points
500 points give one free 250 g pack of any classic blend. Points cannot be exchanged for cash and cannot be combined with festival discount codes.

## Points expiry
Points expire 12 months after they are earned. Expiring points are listed on the account page 30 days before they disappear.

## Tiers
Customers who spend more than 10,000 rupees in a calendar year become Gold members. Gold members get free express delivery and early access to seasonal blends.
