# Customer Support

## Opening hours
Customer support is open Monday to Saturday, 10am to 6pm IST. It is closed on Sundays and on national holidays.

## Contact channels
You can reach support by WhatsApp at +91 90000 12345, by email at help@chaikitchen.example, or through the chat window on the website. Phone calls are not supported.

## Response times
WhatsApp and chat messages are answered within 2 hours during opening hours. Emails are answered within 1 working day.

## Escalation
If a problem is not solved within 3 working days, ask the agent to escalate the ticket. Escalated tickets are handled by the support lead and receive a reply within 24 hours.
